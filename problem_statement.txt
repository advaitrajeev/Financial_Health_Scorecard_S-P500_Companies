Financial Health Scorecard — S&P 500 Companies
================================================

What are we measuring?
----------------------
This project measures the financial health of every company in the S&P 500 index
across five core dimensions:

  1. Profitability  — Does the business actually make money?
  2. Liquidity      — Can it pay its bills in the next 12 months?
  3. Leverage       — How much debt is it carrying?
  4. Efficiency     — How well does it use its assets?
  5. Growth         — Is the business expanding or contracting?

Each company receives a composite Financial Health Score derived from standard
financial ratios within these dimensions. No single metric tells the full story —
a company can look profitable but be drowning in debt, or have great liquidity
but be shrinking. All five lenses are required.

Why does it matter?
-------------------
Financial health analysis is the foundation of equity research, credit analysis,
and investment due diligence. Analysts at every tier — from junior associates at
boutique advisory firms to portfolio managers at BlackRock — spend significant
time assessing whether a company can sustain its operations, service its debt,
and generate returns.

What they don't have is a standardised, reproducible, automated version of that
analysis across 500+ companies simultaneously. That's what this project builds.

The timing matters too. With rising interest rates, higher refinancing costs, and
slowing revenue growth in several sectors post-2022, a tool that quickly surfaces
financially stressed companies is directly relevant to real decisions being made
right now.

Who uses it?
------------
  - Equity research analysts   — screening for distressed companies before
                                  initiating coverage
  - Portfolio managers          — monitoring existing holdings for deteriorating
                                  fundamentals
  - Credit risk teams           — early warning for potential covenant breaches
  - Investment banking associates — quick comp table construction for pitch books
  - Students and junior analysts — learning what healthy vs unhealthy looks like
                                   across sectors

Key ratios computed:
--------------------
  Profitability:  Net Margin, ROE, ROA, Operating Margin
  Liquidity:      Current Ratio, Quick Ratio
  Leverage:       Debt-to-Equity, Interest Coverage Ratio
  Efficiency:     Asset Turnover
  Growth:         Revenue Growth (YoY)

Data sources:
-------------
  - S&P 500 constituent list from Wikipedia
  - Company fundamentals via yfinance (income statement, balance sheet, cash flow)
